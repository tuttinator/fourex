Player-colored regions are painted with magenta #FF00FF as a mask. At runtime, Pixi replaces those pixels with the player's banner color from the heraldic palette below — no shader needed, just a per-frame palette swap or a baked tinted variant. Terrain tiles have no mask; they tile seamlessly.
